The Language of Interfaces

Three talks about how interfaces speak to the people who use them. Frank Chimero asks what screens want, and Nicole Fenton writes about interface writing and about words as material. The text is intentiaonally set in a different era of interface design to tell the story and arguments of each of the articles. The designs are set in early personal computers, the 2000s web, and the way the UI now are made for screens we use now.
